Laboratory lead

Professor Gazala Habib

Understanding air pollution, from its sources to its effects on human health.

Professor Gazala Habib studies atmospheric aerosols, air pollution monitoring and the health and climate implications of particulate matter. Her work connects measurements at emission sources with the chemical and physical properties of particles in the atmosphere and the exposures people experience.

Her research spans ground-based emission inventories, field experiments, source characterization and apportionment, aerosol chemistry, and health assessments using epidemiological studies. This work brings together primary data collection, laboratory analysis and observations of ambient air to inform air quality management.
