Research theme 01

Emission inventories & primary data

Developing bottom-up emission inventories grounded in measurements and surveys. Work on transport, residential fuels and industrial activity links real-world practices with emission factors and spatial estimates.

  • Primary activity data
  • Emission factors
  • Sectoral inventories

Research theme 02

Field experiments & source characterization

Measuring particles and gases close to their sources, including vehicles and biomass combustion. Instrument development and field campaigns examine the physical, chemical and optical properties of emissions under real operating conditions.

  • On-road measurements
  • Combustion experiments
  • Source sampling

+Research photograph · Atmospheric aerosol chemistry

Research theme 03

Atmospheric aerosol chemistry

Understanding particle composition, seasonal variability and atmospheric processing. Measurements of organic aerosol, water-soluble ions and trace constituents help distinguish primary emissions from secondary formation in polluted air.

  • Chemical speciation
  • Atmospheric processing
  • Long-term observations

Reference publications

  1. Contributions of primary sources to submicron organic aerosols in Delhi, India ↗

    Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics · 2022

  2. Submicron aerosol composition in the world's most polluted megacity: the Delhi Aerosol Supersite study ↗

    Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics · 2019

Research theme 04

Source apportionment & air quality management

Combining chemical observations with receptor modelling to identify contributions from different sources. Studies of industrial and urban environments support regional approaches to reducing particulate pollution.

  • Receptor modelling
  • Source attribution
  • Airshed assessment

Reference publications

  1. Source apportionment and potential source regions of size-resolved particulate matter at a heavily polluted industrial city in the Indo-Gangetic Plain ↗

    Atmospheric Environment · 2023

  2. Source apportionment resolved by time of day for improved deconvolution of primary source contributions to air pollution ↗

    Atmospheric Measurement Techniques · 2022

Research theme 05

Exposure, epidemiology & health

Connecting ambient and personal exposure to health indicators, including heart-rate variability and quality of life. Complementary toxicological and risk-assessment studies investigate respiratory deposition, particulate metals and cellular responses.

  • Epidemiological studies
  • Personal exposure
  • Toxicity & health risk

Reference publications

  1. Association between traffic emissions mixed with resuspended dust and heart rate variability among healthy adults in Delhi ↗

    Air Quality, Atmosphere & Health · 2020

  2. PM2.5 exposure in highly polluted cities: A case study from New Delhi, India ↗

    Environmental Research · 2017

Research theme 06

Aerosol optics & climate interactions

Examining how particle composition, size and mixing state affect light absorption and water uptake. Work on carbonaceous aerosols and hygroscopicity connects source measurements with climate-relevant aerosol properties.

  • Light absorption
  • Hygroscopicity
  • Radiative effects

    Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics · 2024

  2. Significant contribution of fractal morphology to aerosol light absorption in polluted environments dominated by black carbon (BC) ↗

    npj Climate and Atmospheric Science · 2024
